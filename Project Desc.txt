a book tracking site made to organize and manage reading lists. The site provides a platform where users can keep track of books they have read, are currently reading, or plan to read in the future. The goal is to provide a way for readers to organize their reading list and maintain an overview of their interests and backlog. The site allows users to add new books to their collection by submitting their information through a form, including the book title, author, genre, and status. Once submitted, each book is saved as a record and displayed in the user's personal collection through a dashboard. This allows users to easily view and keep track of their reading lists. this uses an interface to connect the book submission form with the collection dashboard, allowing newly created records to appear in the displayed collection.
